## Time, taken apart

[Visit Escapement](https://escapement.filed.fyi/) or
[browse its source](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/escapement).

Escapement explains mechanical watches through anatomy, physics, escapement
families, complications, landmark calibres, and history. Its repository records
53 Markdown pages and an eight-part anatomy reading path. This is a complete
publication, not a theme demo padded with placeholder text.

## What La Famille does here

- Turns the Markdown into a self-contained static artifact.
- Uses the editorial layout with system fonts and site-owned SVG diagrams.
- Generates taxonomy, search, backlinks, and a browsable knowledge graph.
- Exports a machine-readable content archive after the HTML build.

The site's source notes record practical friction: manual series ordering,
category normalization, publish ordering for RAG, and root-absolute image paths.
Those notes make it a useful workload for improving the generator, not proof
that every requested publishing feature is solved.

## What to try

Start with [the anatomy series](https://escapement.filed.fyi/anatomy/01-the-problem-of-timekeeping/).
Then follow a link into the physics or an escapement family. Use
[the graph](https://escapement.filed.fyi/graph/) to see how the explanations
connect.
