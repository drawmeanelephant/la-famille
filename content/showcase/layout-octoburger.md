## Layout Octoburger Demo

This page demonstrates the `layout-octoburger` layout, the flagship theme of
the release packet. It carries the 🍔 OCTOBURGER identity from the TUI: a
bun-yellow masthead with sesame-seed highlights, a patty-layered content card,
pink selection rings, and an eight-arm scallop flourish under every title.
