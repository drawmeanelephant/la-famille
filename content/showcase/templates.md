## Find your starting point

Released binaries include a curated theme packet. Run `la-famille themes` to
see what your binary ships, and choose one with `init --theme <name>`.
These are the active theme previews:

- [Octoburger](layout-octoburger.md)
- [Editorial](layout-editorial.md)
