## Editorial

This page demonstrates the `layout-editorial` layout: a serif gazette with a
centered masthead, hairline rules, and a drop cap on the opening paragraph.

The editorial layout is deliberately old-fashioned. It leans on system serif
stacks, generous margins, and print-inspired details instead of framework
styling. Everything renders offline; there is not a single network request.

### Where it shines
- Long-form essays and journals
- Announcements that deserve gravitas
- Sites that want to feel typeset, not templated

> "Typography is what language looks like."
